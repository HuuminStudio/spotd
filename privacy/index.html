Privacy Policy

Spot'd is designed to help you track workouts and connect with people you choose. This policy explains what information the app uses and how you can control it.

Last updated: August 27, 2026

At a glance

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use your information for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.
  • Core workout tracking can be used without creating a Spot'd social account.
  • You control whether workout activity is shared with friends.
  • You can delete your account in the app.

Information Spot'd uses

Workout and routine data

Exercises, routines, sets, repetitions, weights, timers, notes, and workout history are used to provide the workout tracker. This information is primarily stored on your device. If you choose a gym or city, the text you select is saved with the related workout or profile information.

Account and social information

If you use social features, Sign in with Apple provides an account identifier and, when you choose to share them, your name and email address. Spot'd also stores your profile, friend code, friendships, friend requests, and block list so those features work across devices.

Activity you choose to share

Depending on your sharing settings, friends may see summary workout statistics and recent workout sessions. Spot'd limits shared session detail and does not publish your private workout notes as a public feed.

Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes all payments. RevenueCat receives purchase status, product identifiers, transaction information, and an app-specific account identifier so Spot'd can unlock and restore Pro features. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

Notifications and device information

If you enable notifications, Spot'd stores a push-notification device token to deliver requested social and account alerts. Basic technical information may be processed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

Device permissions

Spot'd asks for permission only when a feature needs it. Notifications are optional. Photo-library access is used only when you ask to save a generated report or image. You can change permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

Why we process information

We use information only as needed to:

  • provide workout, account, friend, and Pro features;
  • sync the social information you choose to store;
  • deliver notifications you enable;
  • restore purchases and maintain entitlements;
  • protect the service from abuse and diagnose technical problems; and
  • comply with legal obligations.

Service providers

Spot'd relies on Apple for sign-in, purchases, notifications, and platform services; Supabase for hosted account and social data; and RevenueCat for subscription and entitlement management. These providers process information on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments.

Retention and deletion

On-device workout data remains until you remove it or delete the app, subject to your device backups. Hosted account and social data is kept while your account is active and for only as long afterward as reasonably necessary for security, legal, or operational purposes.

You can permanently delete your social account from Me → Edit Profile → Delete Account. See the account deletion guide for details.

Your choices

You can use workout tracking without signing in, manage sharing within Spot'd, remove friends, block users, disable notifications, restore purchases, and delete your account. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or receive a copy of personal information.

Children and international use

Spot'd is not directed to children under 13, or a higher minimum age where local law requires it. Information may be processed in countries other than your own, with safeguards appropriate to the service provider and applicable law.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as Spot'd evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to support@leanline.app. Spot'd is operated by HuuminStudio.
