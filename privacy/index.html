Privacy Policy

Fitmo is designed to help you track workouts and connect with people you choose. This policy explains what information the app uses and how you can control it.

Last updated: September 10, 2026

At a glance

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use your information for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.
  • Core workout tracking can be used without creating a Fitmo social account.
  • You control whether workout activity is shared with friends.
  • You can delete your account in the app.

Information Fitmo uses

Workout and routine data

Exercises, routines, sets, repetitions, weights, timers, notes, and workout history are used to provide the workout tracker. This information is primarily stored on your device. When you choose a gym or current area, its name and optional map coordinates are saved with the local workout or routine. Imported workout files and photos added only to report designs are processed on your device. A report is shared externally only when you export it through an app or destination you choose.

Account and social information

If you use social features, Sign in with Apple provides an account identifier and, when you choose to share them, your name and email address. Fitmo also stores your profile, friend code, friendships, friend requests, and block list so those features work across devices.

Activity you choose to share

After you sign in, Fitmo stores the summary statistics enabled in your privacy settings. Recent-session sharing is enabled by default for accepted friends and can be changed in Me → Statistics & Privacy. Shared sessions cover the last 14 days and include the workout title, date, duration, and a limited exercise summary. Private set-by-set weights, repetitions, notes, and location are excluded from these shared sessions. Friend relationships, requests, blocks, and encouragement interactions are stored to operate the social features.

Profile photos and exercise submissions

If you choose a profile photo, Fitmo uploads a resized copy to private hosted storage so it can be displayed with your profile. People who can discover or view your profile, including permitted friend-code searches, may see the photo. Exercise or equipment submissions upload the name, brand, notes, and photos you choose to send to a private review queue. Please do not include other people’s personal information or sensitive documents.

Private squads

Squad features store membership, squad settings, cycle history, and cheers. Workout completion and activity-day totals support squad participation. Additional metrics are uploaded only for the metric sharing you enable. These may include exercise minutes and lifting volume. Squad members see the relevant progress within the private group. You can manage metric consent and leave a squad; an owner may need to transfer ownership or archive it first.

Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes all payments. RevenueCat receives purchase status, product identifiers, transaction information, and an app-specific account identifier so Fitmo can unlock and restore Pro features and understand purchase and subscription activity through RevenueCat’s dashboard. RevenueCat can create an anonymous app-specific identifier before you sign in, and it is associated with your Fitmo account when you sign in. Purchase processing may include device, app-version, country/storefront, and network information needed to operate and protect the service. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

Notifications and device information

If you enable notifications, Fitmo stores a push-notification device token to deliver requested social and account alerts. Basic technical information may be processed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

Device permissions

Fitmo uses the system photo picker for images you select for an avatar, exercise submission, or report. Saving an exported image can request permission to add to your photo library. Optional location permission helps find nearby gyms or your current area using Apple Maps and geocoding; Fitmo does not continuously track your location or upload workout coordinates to its social backend. Notifications are optional. You can change permissions in iOS Settings. The current app does not read Apple Health or HealthKit data.

Why we process information

We use information only as needed to:

  • provide workout, account, friend, and Pro features;
  • sync the social information you choose to store;
  • deliver notifications you enable;
  • restore purchases, maintain entitlements, and understand subscription performance;
  • protect the service from abuse and diagnose technical problems; and
  • comply with legal obligations.

Service providers

Fitmo uses Apple for sign-in, purchases, notifications, Maps, and platform services; Supabase for hosted account, social, and submitted-content storage; and RevenueCat for subscription and entitlement management. Resend delivers administrative notifications about the exercise review queue; those notification emails do not include submission content. GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process standard request information such as IP addresses to serve and secure it. Support requests sent by email are handled by our email provider. Providers process information as needed to deliver their services under their applicable terms and privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

On-device workout data remains until you remove it or delete the app, subject to your device backups. Hosted account and social data is kept while needed to provide the features you use. Recent shared sessions are limited to a rolling 14-day window by the app’s synchronization flow. Account deletion removes the account and associated profile, avatar, submissions, and social records through the deletion flow. Limited purchase-event, support, security-log, and provider backup records may remain for billing, abuse prevention, legal obligations, or the provider’s retention period. Deletion cannot recall a report or image you already shared with another person or service.

You can permanently delete your social account from Me → Edit Profile → Delete Account. See the account deletion guide for details.

Your choices

You can use workout tracking without signing in, manage sharing within Fitmo, remove friends, block users, disable notifications, restore purchases, and delete your account. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or receive a copy of personal information.

Children and international use

Fitmo is not directed to children under 13, or a higher minimum age where local law requires it. Information may be processed in countries other than your own, with safeguards appropriate to the service provider and applicable law.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as Fitmo evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to LeanLine@163.com. Fitmo is operated by HuuminStudio.
