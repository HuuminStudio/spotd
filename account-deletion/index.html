Your choice

Delete your account

You can request permanent deletion directly inside Fitmo. No support ticket is required.

Last updated: September 10, 2026

In the app

  1. Open Fitmo and select the Me tab.
  2. Open Edit Profile.
  3. Scroll down and choose Delete Account.
  4. Review the warning, confirm deletion, and complete any requested Sign in with Apple verification.

What is deleted

Deletion removes the Fitmo social account and associated hosted profile and avatar, exercise submissions and their photos, friendships, requests, blocks, shared activity, squad membership and related records, notification tokens, and account entitlement links. The Sign in with Apple connection used by Fitmo is also revoked when the deletion flow completes. Limited billing-event, support, security-log, and provider backup records may remain as explained in the privacy policy. Reports you have already sent to others are outside the deletion flow.

Workout history stored locally on your devices is separate from the social account. Remove local records in Fitmo and delete the app from your devices if you also want to remove those on-device copies. Apple device backups may retain data according to your iCloud backup settings.

Purchases are separate

Deleting your Fitmo account does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription or erase Apple transaction records. Manage an active subscription in iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Apple controls billing records and refund requests.

Cannot access the app?

Email LeanLine@163.com from the address associated with your account. We may request limited information to verify ownership before processing the request.
