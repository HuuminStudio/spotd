Your choice

Delete your account

You can request permanent deletion directly inside Spot'd. No support ticket is required.

Last updated: August 27, 2026

In the app

  1. Open Spot'd and select the Me tab.
  2. Open Edit Profile.
  3. Scroll down and choose Delete Account.
  4. Review the warning, confirm deletion, and complete any requested Sign in with Apple verification.

What is deleted

Deletion removes the Spot'd social account and associated hosted profile, friendship, request, block, shared activity, notification-token, and account-linking data. The Sign in with Apple connection used by Spot'd is also revoked when the deletion flow completes.

Workout history stored locally on your devices is separate from the social account. Remove local records in Spot'd and delete the app from your devices if you also want to remove those on-device copies. Apple device backups may retain data according to your iCloud backup settings.

Purchases are separate

Deleting your Spot'd account does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription or erase Apple transaction records. Manage an active subscription in iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Apple controls billing records and refund requests.

Cannot access the app?

Email support@leanline.app from the address associated with your account. We may request limited information to verify ownership before processing the request.
